Caveats

It lags in bull markets. The edge is avoiding crashes, not out-earning the index; it trails SPY in most strong years.

Bonds are not always a haven. In 2022 Treasuries fell with stocks and the strategy lost about 10%.

Search bias. About ten approaches were tried before this one. It is the simplest textbook rule (Faber, 2007) and sits on a plateau, which limits but doesn't remove the risk of a lucky pick.

Not modelled: taxes on the monthly switches and the bid-ask spread beyond 5 bps per side. Before 2002 the Treasury leg is the VFITX mutual fund; IEF is the tradable ETF from 2002.
