    How the score works

    Each ticker is scored on seven factors, for a total of 0 to 100. A stock at or above the threshold is rated Sustainable.

    FactorMax ptsWhat it measures
    Payout ratio20Share of earnings paid as dividends; lower is safer
    Dividend yield15Sweet spot 3–5%; penalises yield traps above 7%
    Debt / equity15Financial leverage; lower leaves more room to keep paying
    Return on equity15Profitability of the business
    Current ratio10Short-term liquidity
    Free cash flow10Positive FCF is the minimum bar
    5-year dividend CAGR15Dividend growth over the past five years

    Results depend on the completeness of Yahoo Finance's reported figures.
