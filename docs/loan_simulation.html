Total wealth vs. share of salary toward the loan

How it works

Each year, the chosen share of your salary goes to the loan (interest first, then principal) and the rest is invested at the annual return. Once the loan is paid off, the whole salary is invested.

Wealth is the invested amount plus its compounded gains at the end of the horizon, minus anything still owed on the loan. Shares too small to cover the yearly interest plus the fixed installments are skipped as infeasible.

Simulation only. Nothing here is financial advice.
